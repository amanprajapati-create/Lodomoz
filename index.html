Privacy Policy for LudoMoz

Last updated: 16 September 2026

LudoMoz is a Ludo board game for Android. This page explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

LudoMoz ("the app", "we", "us") is developed and published by an independent developer. You can reach us at any time using the contact details at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

Information you give us

LudoMoz does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier in order for you to play.

If you choose to enter a nickname or player name for a game, that name is stored on your own device and is shown to other players only during an online match you take part in. It is not linked to your real identity.

Information collected automatically

When you play an online multiplayer match, our game servers necessarily process limited technical information so that the match can run:

  • An anonymous device or session identifier, used only to keep you connected to the right match
  • Your IP address, which is handled by the connection itself and is not stored for profiling
  • Game state information such as dice rolls, moves, and match results

This information is used solely to operate multiplayer gameplay. It is not sold, rented, or used to build an advertising profile about you.

Information stored on your device

Game settings, sound preferences, and offline game progress are saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your phone unless you back it up yourself using Android's own backup feature, which is controlled by you and by Google, not by us. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state — required to connect to an online multiplayer match and to check whether you are online.
  • Vibration — used only for in-game feedback, if your device supports it and you have not disabled it.

LudoMoz does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, photos, files, or call logs.

Advertising

At present, LudoMoz does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising identifiers.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the Data safety section on our Google Play listing will be updated to match, and the relevant advertising partner and its own privacy policy will be named here.

Purchases

LudoMoz does not currently offer in-app purchases. If purchases are added in future, all payments will be processed by Google Play. We would never receive or store your card, bank, or UPI details.

Children

LudoMoz is suitable for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not require any personal information to play. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

How long information is kept

Match-related technical data is kept only for as long as it is needed to run and complete a game session, and for a short period afterwards to investigate errors or abuse. Data stored locally on your device remains there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Sharing with others

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may be disclosed only where we are legally required to do so, for example in response to a valid legal request from a competent authority.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited information the app handles, including encrypted connections to our game servers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

  • You can stop all data processing at any time by not playing online matches, or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can clear all locally stored game data from Android Settings → Apps → LudoMoz → Storage → Clear data.
  • You can ask us what information relates to your device and request its deletion by writing to the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The revised version will always be posted at this same web address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.
